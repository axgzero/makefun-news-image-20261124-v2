# Where each story in the picture comes from

The picture sums up Tuesday 24 November 2026. I gathered the day's
leads from the morning editions (Reuters first, then AP, AFP and the
Indian press), kept only stories two or more mainstream outlets
carried, and ranked them by how widely they led. Most of the day's
news was actually reported on Monday 23 November in Europe and the
Americas — which is already Tuesday 24 November in Asia-Pacific — and
the one item fixed to Tuesday itself is the CAS decision, due that
afternoon. Where outlets disagreed on a number, the picture uses the
figure most of the day's reporting carried, and quotes are attributed
to whoever said them.

## The six lead stories

### 1. COP31 — the Antalya talks collapse
The UN climate summit in Turkey broke up without a deal: the EU and
others rejected the final text as too weak on fossil fuels, and the
draft said nothing about cutting global demand for them. The EU said
on Monday it was disappointed and would keep pushing the multilateral
process. Read it: Reuters, https://www.reuters.com/sustainability/cop/eu-urges-multilateral-talks-continue-after-cop31-collapse-2026-11-23/ and https://www.reuters.com/sustainability/cop/turkeys-erdogan-says-cop31-laid-groundwork-more-climate-talks-despite-lack-deal-2026-11-23/

### 2. Nvidia — Huang is back as chief executive
Eleven days after resigning, Jensen Huang was reinstated as Nvidia's
CEO on Monday, after pressure from President Trump, who had called
into a board meeting. Shares jumped about 33%, the Nasdaq rallied 6%
and the S&P 500 rose about 4%. Read it: Reuters, https://www.reuters.com/business/view-nvidia-reinstates-jensen-huang-ceo-igniting-tech-surge-2026-11-23/ and https://www.reuters.com/commentary/reuters-open-interest/global-markets-trading-day-graphic-2026-11-23/

### 3. Estonia — Moscow warns of "open war"
Russia's Foreign Ministry accused Estonia of hostile actions "on the
verge of triggering open war" after Tallinn said it would shut its
land border crossings with Russia from 1 December for at least six
months, blaming Russian security services for a staged arson attempt
on an Estonian defence firm. Russia denied any part in the attack.
Read it: Reuters, https://www.reuters.com/world/moscow-accuses-estonia-hostile-actions-verge-triggering-open-war-2026-11-23/

### 4. US politics — the Democrats' biggest House win
Democrats will hold at least 258 seats in the US House — the largest
majority for either party since 1992 — after Chuck Hubbard beat
Republican Virginia Foxx in North Carolina's 5th District. The race
was called on Sunday; the scale of the win was confirmed across
Monday's reporting. Read it: AP via Reflector, https://www.reflector.com/news/national/democrats-clinch-a-historic-house-majority-of-at-least-258-seats-the-largest-since-1992/article_68b13909-fb77-50bd-b68f-6c99e674cf5b.html

### 5. Nepal — $1.62 billion from India
India announced a $1.62 billion economic package for flood-hit Nepal
on Monday, after Modi's talks in New Delhi with Nepali prime minister
Balendra Shah: a $377 million grant plus a $1.24 billion line of
credit, to rebuild after the 26 August glacier flood that killed
about 1,500 people and left 5,200 missing. The two leaders also
opened a new train link together. Read it: Reuters wire via BusinessWorld, https://bworldonline.com/world/2026/11/23/795962/india-to-give-1-62-billion-economic-package-to-flood-hit-nepal/ and The Hindu BusinessLine, https://www.thehindubusinessline.com/economy/india-announces-163-billion-package-for-nepal-marking-pm-balen-shahs-visit/article71734309.ece/amp/

### 6. The ICC — Tajikistan starts its withdrawal
Tajikistan became the fourth country to formally begin leaving the
International Criminal Court since the US launched its diplomatic
push against the court, the presidency of the court's governing body
said on Monday. Leaving takes a year, so the picture says "starts".
Read it: Reuters, https://www.reuters.com/world/asia-pacific/tajikistan-becomes-fourth-country-withdraw-icc-since-us-diplomatic-push-2026-11-23/
