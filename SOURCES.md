## The six headline stories

### 1. COP31 collapses in Antalya
The UN climate talks in Turkey ended in collapse after the EU and
others rejected the final deal as too weak on fossil fuels; the draft
text made no mention of reducing global demand for fossil fuels. On
Monday the EU said it was disappointed by the collapse and would keep
pushing the multilateral process.

- Reuters: https://www.reuters.com/sustainability/cop/eu-urges-multilateral-talks-continue-after-cop31-collapse-2026-11-23/
- Reuters: https://www.reuters.com/sustainability/cop/turkeys-erdogan-says-cop31-laid-groundwork-more-climate-talks-despite-lack-deal-2026-11-23/

### 2. Huang is back at Nvidia
Nvidia reinstated Jensen Huang as chief executive on Monday, only 11
days after he resigned; the reversal followed pressure from President
Trump, who called into a Nvidia board meeting. Nvidia shares jumped
about 33%, the Nasdaq rallied 6% and the S&P 500 rose about 4%.

- Reuters: https://www.reuters.com/business/view-nvidia-reinstates-jensen-huang-ceo-igniting-tech-surge-2026-11-23/
- Reuters Trading Day: https://www.reuters.com/commentary/reuters-open-interest/global-markets-trading-day-graphic-2026-11-23/

### 3. Moscow warns Estonia of "open war"
Russia's Foreign Ministry accused Estonia, a NATO and EU member, of
hostile actions "on the verge of triggering open war" after Estonia
announced it would close its land border crossings with Russia from
1 December for at least six months, accusing Russian security services
of staging an attempted arson attack on an Estonian defence company.
Russia denied involvement in the attack.

- Reuters: https://www.reuters.com/world/moscow-accuses-estonia-hostile-actions-verge-triggering-open-war-2026-11-23/

### 4. Democrats' biggest House win
Democrats won at least 258 seats in the US House after North Carolina
Democrat Chuck Hubbard defeated Republican Virginia Foxx in the 5th
District — the largest House majority held by either party since 1992.
The race was called on Sunday and the majority was confirmed across
Monday's reporting.

- Reuters (podcast round-up): https://www.reuters.com/podcasts/markets-brace-texas-democrats-eye-2028-black-friday-ai-2026-11-23/
- AP via Reflector: https://www.reflector.com/news/national/democrats-clinch-a-historic-house-majority-of-at-least-258-seats-the-largest-since-1992/article_68b13909-fb77-50bd-b68f-6c99e674cf5b.html

### 5. Nepal gets $1.62 billion from India
India announced a $1.62 billion economic package for flood-hit Nepal
on Monday, after Prime Minister Modi's talks in New Delhi with Nepali
Prime Minister Balendra Shah — a $377 million grant plus a $1.24
billion line of credit — to rebuild after the August 26 glacier flood
that killed about 1,500 people and left 5,200 missing. Modi and Shah
also jointly opened a new train link between the two countries.

- Reuters wire: https://bworldonline.com/world/2026/11/23/795962/india-to-give-1-62-billion-economic-package-to-flood-hit-nepal/
- AFP: https://www.nampa.org/text/23072950
- The Hindu BusinessLine: https://www.thehindubusinessline.com/economy/india-announces-163-billion-package-for-nepal-marking-pm-balen-shahs-visit/article71734309.ece/amp/

### 6. Tajikistan starts leaving the ICC
Tajikistan became the fourth country to formally begin withdrawing
from the International Criminal Court since the US launched a
diplomatic push against the court, the presidency of the court's
governing body said on Monday. Withdrawal takes a year to complete;
the image says "starts" and "one-year withdrawal" to match that.

- Reuters: https://www.reuters.com/world/asia-pacific/tajikistan-becomes-fourth-country-withdraw-icc-since-us-diplomatic-push-2026-11-23/

## Method

1. Searched the web for 24 November 2026 world news and collected the
   stories that appeared across multiple independent outlets, keeping
   only stories with mainstream confirmation (Reuters, AFP, The Hindu
   BusinessLine).
2. Ranked them by breadth of coverage and consequence, and kept the
   six that led the morning editions across regions and fields
   (climate, markets, European security, US politics, Asia,
   international justice), with four more in the footer strip.
3. `main.py` draws the image from the stories encoded in it — no
   AI-generated picture, so no garbled text — and the finished image
   was read back and checked headline by headline against the
   reporting above before shipping.
